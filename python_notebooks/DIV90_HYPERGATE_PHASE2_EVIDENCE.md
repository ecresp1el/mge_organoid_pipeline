# DIV90 Hypergate phase 2: developmental programs, metadata, and reagent evidence

Evidence checked: 2026-09-09. This is new supporting documentation; the completed phase-one definitions and results remain unchanged. New runtime results belong under `/nfs/turbo/umms-parent/mgeo_neuron_scrnaseq_projectfolder/results/div90_hypergate_sst_pv_phase2/`.

## Interpretation and recommended modules

These modules measure **development-associated transcriptional programs within the frozen cortical LHX6+/ERBB4+ population**, not established fates. They are separate axes. A high score on both axes is initially an operational dual-high state; neither shared transcription factors nor a median split alone establishes a distinct hybrid cell type. Independent gene-level support, score sensitivity, and technical controls determine how much biological interpretation is warranted.

The final primary PV module is `MEF2C, KCNC1, KCNC2, TAC1`; TAC1 is detected in 208/4,768 cells (4.36%). The final primary SST module is `SST, SATB1, NR2F2, CDK14, CACNG3`. The runtime manifest records detection, normalization and cutoffs. These choices deliberately do not carry the generic GAD1/GAD2 weight from the previous module definition. Do not silently replace phase-one scores when comparing runs.

| Gene | Recommended use | Evidence and specific limitation |
|---|---|---|
| MEF2C | Primary PV axis | An experimentally supported early PV-associated regulator; embryonic mouse PV precursors and loss-of-function evidence support its use. It is not exclusively expressed or utilized by PV cells. [Mayer et al., 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature25999) |
| KCNC1, KCNC2 | Primary PV axis | Kv3 channel genes supply a maturation/fast-spiking component independent of a single transcription factor. A developing fast-spiking interneuron study links their increasing expression with electrophysiological maturation. This is PV-associated physiology, not a fate-specific early progenitor test. [Okaty et al., 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2749660/) |
| TAC1 | Primary PV axis; detected in 208 cells | Used as a PV marker in a developmental MGE cortical interneuron multiomic study, including Extended Data Fig. 10g. It is not required for all PV subtypes or for immature cells. [Allaway et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34552240/) |
| SST | Primary SST axis | Direct neuropeptide evidence, interpreted together with the other components. It receives one gene's weight, not a separate override; assess removal of SST from the score. [Denaxa et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3607226/) |
| SATB1 | Primary SST axis with explicit shared-maturation caveat | Mouse gain/loss experiments support SST-group differentiation and maturation. SATB1 also relates to broader MGE maturation and neuronal activity, so it cannot validate hybrid identity by itself. [Denaxa et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3607226/) |
| NR2F2 | Primary SST axis, sensitivity without it | Within mouse MGE progenitors, Nr2f1/Nr2f2 promote a temporal SST output and repress PV output. This restricted context supports an SST-associated component after cortical/MGE entry gating; it does not make NR2F2 a universal cortical SST marker. [Hu et al., 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5560044/) |
| CDK14 | Primary SST axis, exploratory developmental component | Reported among early SST-associated genes in MGE progenitors (Fig. 4C). The association is mouse embryonic and does not independently establish human DIV90 SST commitment. [Mi et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6195193/) |
| CACNG3 | Primary SST axis, withheld from gate features | An SST marker used in developmental cortical interneuron analysis (Extended Data Fig. 10g). Its membrane association means it must not also act as a discovery feature for states it helped define. [Allaway et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34552240/) |

Human-to-mouse translation is an explicit limitation of this literature-derived module, particularly for early markers. Human and mouse PV interneurons do share KCNC1/KCNC2-associated electrophysiological features, but a score in an organoid is not proof of the mature phenotype. [Regulation of input excitability in human and mouse PV interneurons, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12047855/)

### Candidates retained as controls or sensitivity analyses

- **MAF and MAFB:** inspect both genes and use a broad PV-associated sensitivity module if useful, but omit them from the compact primary PV module. Pai et al. support their promotion of Mef2c and PV differentiation, while Mi et al. find embryonic Maf promotes SST output, and Lim et al. show Mafb regulates SST Martinotti development. Thus the phase-one genes are biologically relevant but context dependent. [Pai et al., 2020](https://elifesciences.org/articles/54903), [Mi et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6195193/), [Lim et al., 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0162-9)
- **ELMO1 and IGFBP4:** optional expanded sensitivity genes, not automatically added to inflate module size. The proposed immature PV marker validation is strongest for mouse hippocampal interneurons; cortical human DIV90 specificity is unestablished. [Pai et al., 2020](https://elifesciences.org/articles/54903)
- **TMEM91:** an alternative SST sensitivity component; it is among the SST marker genes in Allaway et al. Extended Data Fig. 10g. Do not add every plausible marker simply to increase gene count. [Allaway et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34552240/)
- **GRIK1:** retain the exact phase-one SST module for compatibility comparisons, but omit GRIK1 from the primary version. Primary cortical receptor analyses show expression across both SST and PV interneurons; only 91/4,768 cells detect it here. [Structural and compositional diversity in the kainate receptor family, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8581553/)
- **ST18:** visualize as context, not a primary cortical PV score component. An early putative PV association is outweighed by the specificity concern from subsequent experimental work demonstrating a globus pallidus projection-neuron program. [St18 specifies globus pallidus projection neuron identity in MGE lineage, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9751150/)
- **ERBB4:** retain as entry/surface feature and visualization. It must not contribute to the developmental score when its enrichment is being tested. **LHX6, SOX6, GAD1/GAD2** are not major discriminating score components because this population is already selected for cortical MGE/interneuron identity. **PVALB** is not required, and its three detected cells cannot define a prospective population.

MEF2C deserves particular caution even in the compact module: experimental chromatin work identifies both shared and subtype-specific MEF2C regulation. Dual-high RNA therefore supports coexistence of chosen programs, not proof that two mutually exclusive future fates are competing. [Allaway et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9316417/)

NR2F2 also has CGE/subpallial associations. Human interneuron comparisons identify NR2F2/PROX1 among CGE-associated regulators while SATB1/MAF/LHX6 characterize MGE SST comparisons. Preserve the existing cortical selection and separately report NR2F2's contribution; do not reinterpret NR2F2 alone as SST identity. [Allison et al., 2021](https://plathlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/2021-allison-1-s2.0-S2213671121004240-main.pdf)

### Minimum score and hybrid validation

The primary analysis should retain every cell, independent PV/SST continuous scores, and all component expression. Equal gene contributions after a documented transformation limit SST weighting; they do not guarantee that SST cannot dominate observed score variance. Check actual gene contributions and leave-one-gene-out state stability, especially leave-SST-out and leave-MEF2C-out. Compare the primary compact PV module against the broad `MEF2C, MAF, MAFB, KCNC1, KCNC2` sensitivity and the original phase-one definition without altering it.

Show detected-gene counts in each module and dual-high cells with at least two detected genes per module; add the stricter requirement of at least two **non-SST** SST-program genes. Distinguish a supported subset from all operational dual-high cells. Report observed co-detection, dropout limitations, score dependence on nCount/nFeature, mitochondrial fraction, cell-cycle phase and stress. Count-based depth checks and sample-stratified controls are useful sensitivity analyses; cell-line effects should remain visible. Absence of a saved doublet score is unavailable evidence, not a negative doublet result. A de novo computational doublet screen must be explicitly labeled and cannot prove single-cell biological coexpression.

CV/MW and glucose are confounded with operator/other culture differences, and each line has one sample in each condition. Compare CV versus MW within each of H9, 79B and 2E, show all three descriptive paired differences, and retain cell counts. Do not treat 4,768 cells as independent biological replicates or describe a glucose-specific causal effect. Original rep1/rep2 labels are preserved but the newly supplied metadata prevents treating them as exchangeable within-condition biological replicates.

`div90_hypergate_phase2_conditions.py` reports pooled condition effects alongside line-specific pairs. Raw condition differences in a marker can arise from changed state proportions, altered expression within the same operational state, or both. The equal-four-state standardized means remove observed state-mixture differences descriptively; missing state support yields an undefined standardized value, not zero. Within-condition marker/state contrasts and their CV-minus-MW differences assess whether the association itself shifts. These comparisons do not regress away culture biology, establish causation, or eliminate residual technical/sample differences.

Manufacturer documentation was inspected as primary evidence about products. Product listing, extracellular immunogen, nonpermeabilized flow performance, and successful sorting of these DIV90 neurons are separate evidence levels. No clone below is established here as a validated DIV90 organoid sorting reagent. No antibody was ordered and no experimental fluorescence cutoff has been inferred from RNA.

The final constrained RNA candidate is **remove FGFR2 > 1.2491154 OR PTPRS > 3.4076235** inside the frozen entry population. A concrete first protein pilot can therefore examine ERBB4 182818, FGFR2 98739 and PTPRS AF3430 using compatible direct conjugates/secondaries and viability controls. Because ERBB4 and FGFR2 candidates are both mouse antibodies, unseparated anti-mouse secondary staining would not distinguish them; direct conjugation or otherwise validated species/isotype-specific detection is required. Clone 98739 binds a functional receptor domain, while PTPRS antibody crosslinking altered pDC activation in the primary paper; evaluate survival and maturation instead of assuming staining is functionally inert. Surface PTPRS can also internalize or shed, so the pilot must test dissociation/staining effects. No fluorescence threshold or benefit in neurons is established by these sources. [FGFR2 manufacturer](https://www.rndsystems.com/products/human-fgfr2-antibody-98739_mab6843), [Primary PTPRS surface and functional evidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547994/)

For a first prospective experiment, separate the **RNA-selected sorting hypothesis** from a **protein pilot**. Validate extracellular staining, dissociation tolerance, viable singlets and post-sort survival, then choose fluorescence thresholds using actual signal distributions/controls and evaluate recovered fractions. The RNA rule `FAT3 <= 0.764341 AND PTPRM <= 0` predicts a low/undetected transcript fraction; it supplies neither a fluorescence threshold nor evidence of absent protein. If new discovery favors a different marker, perform the same clone/epitope/application audit before calling the sort implementation ready.

The informative maturation comparison is retained versus removed fractions plus an unsorted ERBB4+ control, maintained under matched culture conditions with cell-line/replicate labels preserved. Measure immediate program composition to test the predicted enrichment, then assess multiple maturation endpoints, including PVALB/SST protein and fast-spiking electrophysiology, while counting survival and absolute yields. This tests whether initial program enrichment predicts later phenotype; it does not assume hybrid cells are necessarily intermediates.
